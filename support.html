FAQ

Do I need an account? No. There is no sign-in at all; everything stays on your iPhone.

Where is my data? Only on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes it.

What's free, and what does Plus add? Free: 1 group with 1 bill (scan, itemize, split and settle it fully; deleting the bill doesn't give it back), and 1 Quick Split every 7 days. Spliva Plus is a one-time $4.99 purchase (no subscription) that unlocks unlimited bills, groups and Quick Splits, plus PDF and spreadsheet (CSV) export. No ads.

How do I restore Plus? Settings → Restore Purchases.

Why don't totals look like an even split? Tax and tip are split in proportion to what each person ordered, and totals always add up to the cent.

Scanning missed an item? Every scanned item can be edited, and you can add items by hand.
