Privacy Policy — Spliva

Effective: October 15, 2026 · Contact: mario.novoa1@icloud.com

Spliva ("the app") is built to work without collecting your personal data.

What we collect

Nothing. We do not run servers for the app, and the app contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. We cannot see your bills, groups, names, or receipts.

Data stored on your device

Bills, groups, people's names, optional payment handles (Venmo, Cash App, PayPal usernames), and receipt images/text you choose to save are stored only in the app's local storage on your device. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Camera and photos

If you scan a receipt, the camera or the photo you pick is used only to read text on your device using Apple's on-device Vision framework. Images are not uploaded anywhere.

No account

The app has no sign-in or account. We never receive an identifier for you or your device.

Purchases

The one-time Plus purchase is processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment details. The app checks your purchase status with Apple on the device.

Payment apps

Tapping a Venmo, Cash App, or PayPal button opens that service's app or website with an amount filled in. Anything you do there is governed by that service's privacy policy.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date.
